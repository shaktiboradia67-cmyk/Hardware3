## 📊 Comparison Table

| 🔍 Feature                       | 🟠 DDR3                      | 🟢 DDR4                         | 🟣 DDR5                        |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ⚡ **Typical Speed**              | 800–2133 MT/s                | 1600–3200 MT/s                  | 4800–8800+ MT/s                |
| 🔋 **Voltage**                   | ~1.5 V                       | ~1.2 V                          | ~1.1 V                         |
| 💾 **Typical Capacity / Module** | Up to 8–16 GB                | Up to 32–128 GB                 | Up to 48–256 GB+               |
| 🚀 **Main Advantage**            | Affordable for older systems | Better performance & efficiency | Higher bandwidth & capacity    |
| 🖥️ **Typical Use Cases**        | Older PCs & laptops          | Mainstream PCs & laptops        | New PCs, gaming & workstations |
| 🔌 **Compatibility**             | DDR3 motherboards            | DDR4 motherboards               | DDR5 motherboards              |

## ⭐ Comparison Highlights

### 🟠 DDR3

* 💰 Suitable for older, budget systems
* ⚡ Speed range: **800–2133 MT/s**
* 🔋 Higher operating voltage than newer generations
* 🖥️ Mostly found in older PCs and laptops

### 🟢 DDR4

* ⚡ Faster and more efficient than DDR3
* 🔋 Lower voltage: **~1.2 V**
* 🖥️ Widely used in mainstream computers
* ⚖️ Good balance between performance and cost

### 🟣 DDR5

* 🚀 Starts at much higher speeds: **4800 MT/s+**
* 🔋 Lower voltage: **~1.1 V**
* 💾 Supports higher memory capacities
* 🎮 Well suited for modern gaming PCs and demanding workloads

## ⚠️ Compatibility Note

> **DDR3, DDR4, and DDR5 RAM are not interchangeable.**

Always check your **motherboard's specifications** before purchasing RAM. The motherboard must support the specific DDR generation.

## 🏆 Quick Takeaway

**DDR3 → Older systems** 🖥️
**DDR4 → Mainstream systems** 💻
**DDR5 → Modern high-performance systems** 🚀

---

### 📌 Key Point

As the DDR generation increases, memory generally provides **higher bandwidth, lower operating voltage, and greater capacity**, although actual performance depends on the CPU, motherboard, memory configuration, and workload.

Ans. # 🧠 RAM Installation & Verification

## 🔧 Objective

To locate the RAM module, safely remove and reinstall it, and verify that the computer boots successfully and recognizes the installed memory.

## 📋 Procedure

1. 🔌 Shut down the computer and disconnect the power supply.
2. 🛡️ Open the desktop case or laptop back panel carefully.
3. 🔍 Locate the **RAM slots** on the motherboard.
4. 📸 Take a clear photo of the installed RAM module.
5. ↔️ Release the RAM module by opening the retaining clips on both sides.
6. 🧤 Carefully remove the RAM by holding it from the edges.
7. 🔄 Align the notch on the RAM module with the key in the RAM slot.
8. ⬇️ Press the RAM module firmly and evenly until the retaining clips lock it into place.
9. 🔌 Close the case/panel and reconnect the power supply.
10. 💻 Turn on the computer and verify that the system boots normally.
11. ✅ Check the system information or **Task Manager → Performance → Memory** to confirm that the RAM is detected correctly.
